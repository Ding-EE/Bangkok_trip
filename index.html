6 floors of toys, cameras, and gaming. Great for finding rare items.
Location

Take a Tuk-Tuk from Mega Plaza (~100 THB). See the massive gold Buddha. Then cross river (5 THB) to Wat Arun.
Entry: 300 THB

Chinatown Street Food
Yaowarat Road. The neon lights turn on and food stalls take over the street.

Terminal 21 (Asok)

Day 4: MBK & Escape
11:00 AM

Souvenir haven. Dried mango, elephant pants, and cheap electronics accessories.
