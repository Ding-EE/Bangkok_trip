Getting Around

🚕 Grab / Bolt:
Since we are 4 people, using the Bolt App is often cheaper than 4 train tickets! Plus, it's comfy A/C door-to-door.

Rush Hour Alert!
Avoid cars between 5:00 PM - 7:30 PM. Use the BTS Skytrain instead!

Money Tips

  • 💳 Cards: Use Wise or GXBank for the best rates.
  • 💵 Cash: Bring some MYR cash to exchange at SuperRich (orange/green booths) for the best rate.
  • 🏧 ATM Warning: Thai ATMs charge ~220 THB (RM28) per withdrawal! Withdraw big amounts at once.

Best Areas to Stay

🏆 Phaya Thai Area

Best for airport access! Direct train line to BKK airport and close to shopping.

🚇 Asok / Sukhumvit

The "Center of Everything". Where the Skytrain meets the Subway. Very convenient.

👗 Pratunam

Best for street food & cheap clothes right outside your door.
