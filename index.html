Money & Sim
  • 💳 Card: Wise/GXBank work everywhere (7-11, Malls).
  • 💵 Cash: Bring MYR to exchange at SuperRich (Green/Orange booths).
  • 📲 Sim: Buy "Travel Sim" at any 7-Eleven (~200 THB). Cheaper than Airport.
Scam Alerts
The "Closed" Scam: If a Tuk-Tuk driver says the Grand Palace is "closed for a holiday," ignore them. It's open.
