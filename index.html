Getting Around (For a Family of 4)

  • Grab vs. Bolt: For a family of 4, ride-hailing is often cheaper than 4 separate train tickets. Bolt is usually the cheapest option, but Grab has a larger fleet with more "Large/XL" options for luggage.
  • Traffic Warning: Always use the BTS/MRT during rush hours (7:30–9:30 AM and 5:00–7:30 PM) to avoid being stuck in gridlock.
  • BTS Skytrain: Very efficient. If you have younger kids, note that children under 90cm ride for free.
Airport Transfer: If landing at Suvarnabhumi (BKK), the Airport Rail Link is fast (45 THB/pax), but booking a Grab directly from the airport is highly recommended for a family of 4 to avoid hauling luggage up and down station stairs.

Currency & Payments

Cash is still king for street food and small markets.


  • You can withdraw Thai Baht from local ATMs using your GXBank or Wise card. Note: All Thai ATMs charge a flat 220 THB (~RM28) fee per transaction, so withdraw the maximum amount allowed in one go to minimize fees.
  • Alternatively, bring MYR cash and exchange it at a green or orange SuperRich booth in the city for the best exchange rates.

Recommended Areas for Families

1. Phaya Thai / Ratchathewi (Top Choice)

Directly on the Airport Rail Link line, making arrival and departure incredibly easy. Just one or two BTS stops from major shopping districts.

2. Asok / Sukhumvit

The intersection of the BTS (Skytrain) and MRT (Subway). Staying here means you rarely have to transfer lines, saving a lot of walking for the family.

3. Pratunam

Famous for wholesale clothing markets and cheap, excellent street food right outside your door. Highly walkable to CentralWorld.
