Wat Pho (Reclining Buddha)
Take a Tuk-Tuk from Mega Plaza (~100 THB). See the massive gold Buddha. Then cross river (5 THB) to Wat Arun.
Entry: 300 THB
6:30 PM
Chinatown Street Food
Yaowarat Road. The neon lights turn on and food stalls take over the street.

10:30 AM
E-Gokart (MONOWHEEL)
Located at Block 28 (Near Chula). High-speed electric karts. Air-conditioned lounge for parents if not racing.
⚡ ~450 THB/race
Navigate
1:00 PM
Terminal 21 (Asok)
Head to Pier 21 (5th Floor). Best value food court. Exchange cash for a card at the counter.

Clean, modern market. Famous for "Leng Saeb" (Spicy Mountain Ribs). Great for a family feast.
